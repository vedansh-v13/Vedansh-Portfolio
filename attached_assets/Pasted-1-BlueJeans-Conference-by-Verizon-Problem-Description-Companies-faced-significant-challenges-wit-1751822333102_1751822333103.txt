1. BlueJeans Conference by Verizon
🧩 Problem Description
Companies faced significant challenges with hybrid and virtual meetings—specifically:

Inconsistent user experience across devices (desktop, mobile, conference rooms).

Lack of inclusive features for global teams (e.g., language support, accessible interfaces).

Difficulty in hosting large-scale interactive events with high engagement.

📈 Pain Points
Low engagement in virtual events.

Poor video/audio quality across platforms.

Inability to support diverse participants (language barriers, accessibility).

Manual meeting setups and suboptimal presenter experiences.

🚀 Solution
Redesigned and enhanced the BlueJeans Meetings and Events platform to deliver:

Multi-device, high-quality audio/video collaboration.

Inclusive features like multi-language captions and auto-framing.

Scalable event capabilities enabling up to 150,000 interactive participants.

Frictionless presenter tools: virtual background, auto-camera framing, “weather person mode”.

✅ Key Features Implemented
Cross-platform HD video and content sharing (desktop, mobile, Portal)
