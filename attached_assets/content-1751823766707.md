#### What is it?




UCCaaS is a managed unified communications service offering scalable call control, voicemail, instant messaging, presence and mobility for small businesses, government agencies and enterprise organizations.

- ![](https://d23uobcja6cuc.cloudfront.net/business/dam/img/products/voice-collaboration/unified-communications/unified-communications-and-collaboration-as-a-service/uccaas-how-600x260.jpg)

#### How does it help you?




UCCaaS helps improve operational efficiency and customer touch points, integrating seamlessly with systems through application programming interfaces (APIs). It’s delivered from Verizon’s private cloud over our secure, reliable global IP network.


## UCCaaS features and benefits

- Scale with your business and deploy more quickly.

- Focus your IT on core projects that improve your competitive advantage.

- Limit expenditures by buying only what you need, when you need it.

- Benefit from predictable per-user, per-month pricing.

- Give your teams a host of functionality, including voice calling, voicemail and instant messaging.

- Ensure protected communications so you don’t have to worry about security.

- Provide another layer of security and reliability for government services with the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP).

- Tailor our services to your needs, making migration and adoption easy for your business.
